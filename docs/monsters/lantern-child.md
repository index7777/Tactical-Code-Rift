## Identity and teaching role

- role: small, high-speed yokai; teaches speed and 截刀
- visual read: child-sized figure under a torn straw hat, long wet black hair, one cold eye, red cords and bells, oversized paper lantern
- combat scale: approximately 0.72–0.82× ordinary player combat height; clearly smaller than 濡骸

## Palette

- straw: muted tan / wet brown
- cloth: charcoal blue-grey with restrained red cord accents
- skin: cold grey-pale
- lantern: aged cream paper, dark frame, small warm ember core
- spirit fire: blue-white only as runtime FX, never baked into the reusable sprite

## Body, face and costume rules

- hunched, floating or toe-light stance; feet remain readable for the shared ground pivot
- face is mostly hidden by hair and hat; one small eye accent may be retained
- torn layered robe and straw hat form the main silhouette; red cords and bells are secondary accents
- lantern is the signature prop and must remain clearly separated from the torso

## Weapon / prop dimensions

- no conventional weapon; one oversized paper lantern held on the outer hand
- lantern width approximately 0.30–0.36× body height
- handle and tassel remain visible in side-view; do not replace with staff or sword

## Silhouette and facing

- strict 2D side-view, facing toward the battlefield center
- horizontal flip allowed only after checking lantern hand, cord asymmetry and eye placement
- no 8-direction or frontal combat sprite
- feet / lowest cloth edge define pivot; no baked ground, shadow, text, UI or FX

## Allowed variations

- idle sway, ready lean, hit recoil and down pose through runtime transforms first
- lantern ember brightness and separate blue spirit-fire FX
- small bell swing and cloth flutter

## Forbidden variations

- baked blue flames, bloom, cast shadow or background halo
- adult proportions, large monster scale, extra weapons or armor
- changing lantern into a generic lamp or removing the straw silhouette
