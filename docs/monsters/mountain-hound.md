## Identity and teaching role

- role: fast, medium-low damage pressure unit; teaches high-speed 殺生線 pressure
- visual read: wet black mountain dog, oversized mane, pale exposed limbs, red yokai fissures, shrine tags and bells
- combat scale: approximately 0.85–0.95× ordinary player combat height; wider horizontal silhouette, not a giant monster

## Palette

- fur: charcoal / blue-black, low saturation
- exposed skin: cold grey
- yokai fissures: restrained dark crimson; separate runtime FX may add a brief red pulse
- tags: aged warm paper with muted vermilion marks
- eyes: small red-orange focal points, never a full-screen glow

## Anatomy and costume rules

- quadruped crouch with the head and forequarters leading the silhouette
- mane must read as wet, spiked and heavy; tail remains visible as a single sweeping mass
- shrine tags and bell cord are the only adornments
- claws and muzzle are sharp but remain readable at 82–100 px battle scale

## Silhouette and facing

- strict 2D side-view, head toward the battlefield center
- horizontal flip allowed; preserve the tag/bell grouping after flip
- no frontal, 3/4 or eight-direction combat sprite
- feet/claw contact points define the pivot; no baked ground, shadow, text, UI or FX

## Allowed variations

- idle crouch, ready lunge, hit recoil and down through runtime transforms first
- separate red fissure pulse, eye glint and dust/rain impact FX
- tail sway and mane flutter as low-cost runtime motion

## Forbidden variations

- giant wolf/boss scale, armor, extra heads, wings or weapon props
- baked red aura, smoke, blood, ground shadow or background halo
- changing quadruped anatomy into a humanoid yokai
