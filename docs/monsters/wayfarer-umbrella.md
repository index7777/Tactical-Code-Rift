## Identity and teaching role

- role: slow, high-power unit; teaches whether to meet a heavy attack head-on
- visual read: enormous torn umbrella canopy, hidden face with one red eye, paper talismans, bells and long pale limbs
- combat scale: approximately 1.05–1.20× ordinary player combat height; wide canopy silhouette but not an elite/boss body

## Palette

- canopy: wet charcoal, bone paper and restrained vermilion wear
- exposed limbs: cold grey-pale
- talismans: aged paper with muted red ink
- eye / yokai accent: small red focal point; red smoke is runtime FX only

## Anatomy and costume rules

- umbrella canopy is the primary identity mass and must stay readable in side-view
- face remains hidden except for one eye slit
- long limbs and torn streamers hang beneath the canopy; bells and talismans are secondary details
- no conventional handheld weapon; the canopy and body drive the heavy attack silhouette

## Silhouette and facing

- strict 2D side-view, canopy and limbs facing the battlefield center
- horizontal flip allowed after checking talisman placement and eye slit
- no frontal, 3/4 or eight-direction combat sprite
- feet define pivot; no baked ground, shadow, text, UI or FX

## Allowed variations

- slow hover / sway, ready compression, hit recoil and down through runtime transforms first
- separate red smoke, eye pulse, paper flutter and bell impact FX
- heavy attack may use a canopy sweep FX, never repaint the master

## Forbidden variations

- small normal umbrella, human face, sword or extra weapon
- baked red smoke, bloom, aura, ground shadow or background halo
- oversized boss scale or a different umbrella pattern
