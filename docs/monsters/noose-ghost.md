## Identity

- role: first-world hexer-line yokai; teaches balance/status pressure and delayed threat
- visual read: hanging strangled figure, pale limp body, dark hemp rope coiled around neck and shoulders, drifting hair
- scale: 1.0–1.1× ordinary player combat height; never oversized elite silhouette

## Palette

- rope / cord: raw hemp with damp brown-black shading
- cloth: bruised indigo with cold grey highlights
- skin: pale corpse-blue, hint of violet at extremities
- eye accent: single dim red-white point only; do not make both eyes glow
- shadows: cool violet, no warm brown or blood-red baked in

## Body / face / costume rules

- head tilted forward and to one side; rope tension is the main silhouette read
- limbs limp and slightly asymmetric; feet dangling with no ground contact
- torn ceremonial robe / hemp sash; do not add samurai armor or clean tailoring
- hair falls over the eyes; keep the mouth region shadowed, no exposed anime face

## Weapon dimensions

- primary "weapon" is the rope itself — used as flail / snare
- optional small talisman charm on rope; no swords or spears
- rope length ≈ 1.3× body height so it reads as reach even in idle

## Silhouette and facing

- strict 2D side-view, facing toward the battlefield center
- safe horizontal flip only if rope loop, tilt and dangling foot asymmetry remain visually coherent
- no 8-direction, 3/4 or frontal combat sprite
- rope is part of the actor; do not bake ground shadow, floor rain, FX or UI

## Allowed variations

- idle, ready, attack, hit, break and down through runtime transforms / FX first
- rope sway, hair drift and small breathing / spasm loop
- attack pose may swing the rope forward while preserving hanging silhouette

## Forbidden variations

- exposed anime face, clean features, oversized giant body
- blood or noose burn baked into the reusable master
- new weapons, magic circles, glowing runes, cast shadow, ground splash
- changing the rope silhouette or muting the neck-tilt asymmetry
