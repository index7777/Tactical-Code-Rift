## Identity

- role: first-world hexer-line yokai; teaches delay / status disruption in mid-tier fights
- visual read: pilgrim monk gone astray in the rain; wide-brim straw hat, dark travel robes, wooden staff with jangling rings
- scale: 1.0× ordinary player combat height; never oversized elite silhouette

## Palette

- robe: soaked charcoal-indigo with cold desaturated tone
- hat: worn straw ochre, darker where the rain has drenched it
- staff: aged wood brown with a dull brass ring cluster at the top
- skin: pale grey-green with a hollow undertone; keep hands and neck visible but not colorful
- eye accent: single small pale amber point; no dual glow

## Body / face / costume rules

- upright but slightly hunched posture; the hat brim hides the eyes and upper face
- staff held in one hand; rings at the top of the staff read as the secondary silhouette accent
- prayer bead loop or torn hemp sash allowed at the waist; no armor plates
- feet in worn straw sandals; no glowing footprints or magic aura

## Weapon dimensions

- one long wooden staff (錫杖 / khakkhara) with brass rings at the top
- staff length ≈ 1.15× body height in the master silhouette
- weapon remains in the same hand; do not swap to sword, sickle or paired weapons

## Silhouette and facing

- strict 2D side-view, facing toward the battlefield center
- safe horizontal flip only if hat asymmetry, staff hand and beard/bead layout remain visually coherent
- no 8-direction, 3/4 or frontal combat sprite
- staff and hat are part of the actor; do not bake ground, rain, magic light or UI onto the master

## Allowed variations

- idle, ready, attack, hit, break and down through runtime transforms / FX first
- gentle staff sway, robe drift, ring jingle timing and pale eye flicker
- attack pose may thrust or swing the staff forward while preserving the hat and hunched posture

## Forbidden variations

- exposed anime face, clean shaven bright-eyed monk, oversized body
- blood, chant runes or ring glow baked into the reusable master
- new weapons (sword, naginata, sickle), magic circles, cast shadow, ground splash
- removing the hat silhouette or straightening the posture to a hero pose
