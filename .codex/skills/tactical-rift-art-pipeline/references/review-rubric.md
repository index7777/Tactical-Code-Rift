# Screenshot review rubric

## Character

- Identity: face, hair landmarks and age impression match the master.
- Construction: head/body ratio, costume layers and weapon dimensions do not drift.
- Runtime: silhouette survives 82 px; feet share the pivot; no halo, crop or neighbor overlap.
- Direction: horizontal flip is allowed only when asymmetric details remain valid.

## Background

- Area identity and palette match the Area Art Bible.
- Central 45–50% remains quieter than both outer staging zones.
- All eight feet read as grounded across Y-axis lanes.
- The intended location reads without actors, UI, intent lines or baked FX.
